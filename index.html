Study Habits, Sleep and Marks

A 200-student implementation study on simulated data.

Problem Method Results Graphs Data Limits

The data is simulated on purpose. Real student data needs consent and could identify people, so every result here follows from rules written in advance. Nothing is evidence about real students.

Problem and objectives

Teachers see marks, attendance and study or sleep habits in separate places, so they cannot say which habits go with better marks. This project joins them in one pipeline.

  • Put study, sleep, attendance and marks in one table.
  • Measure how they relate without over-claiming.
  • Build one easy Habit Score.
  • Group students into Low, Medium and High marks.
  • Show everything in simple graphs.

Method

Specify

Write the rules for how habits affect marks.

Generate

Create 200 students with seed 2026 and save a CSV.

Index

Combine 3 habits into a 0 to 100 Habit Score.

Analyse

Correlation, regression, groups, sleep bands.

Visualise

Three graphs of the results.

The marks rule

marks = 30 + 4 × study + 0.25 × attendance + 1.5 × sleep − 0.8 × screen + noise

A hidden trait, discipline, nudges every habit. It is never saved in the table, which is why habits and marks look linked.

Habit Score weights (sum = 1.0)

Study 0.4 (6 hours a day is full marks), attendance 0.3, sleep 0.3 (4 hours is 0, 8 hours is 100). The weights are fixed in advance so the score is transparent.

What it shows

  • Study shows the biggest difference: the High group studies about 4.02 hours a day against 1.01 in the Low group.
  • Marks rise with sleep, but sleep adds only 1.5 marks per hour in the rule, so its effect is smaller than study.
  • The Low group has only 9 students, so its averages are not very reliable.

Limitations and next step

  • The data is simulated, so the numbers say nothing about real students.
  • Nothing here proves cause. Even on real data, better students may simply sleep more.
  • The Habit Score weights are a judgement. Changing them changes the results.
  • Next step: collect real, consented data with the same columns and run the same analysis.
